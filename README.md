## Why this guide

Subscribing to ChatGPT Plus from mainland China is harder than it should be:

- **Payment restrictions** — most Chinese-issued cards are declined by OpenAI/Stripe.
- **Region restrictions** — OpenAI does not officially serve mainland China; IP and account risk control apply.
- **Error messages are opaque** — "Your card was declined" tells you nothing about the actual cause.

This guide collects what actually works in 2026: real payment paths, real error cases, and real fixes. Updated as policies and models change.
